Small Town Lanes

A rebrand concept for Canajoharie, NY's bowling alley, pro shop, and attached grill & sports bar (currently known as Iroquois Lanes & Pro Shop, reachable only by phone and scattered directory listings). This is a demo built to show what a real site under a new name could look like, not the business's official site. It hasn't been adopted by the business, and details shown here, including the name, may still change.

The Brief

Book a party without playing phone tag

Bowling alleys make real money on things a website can drive directly: birthday parties, league nights, and glow bowling. The site is built around that — a clear "book your event" path, bowling & leagues info, and the grill's menu treated as a first-class page, not an afterthought.

This is a pitch demo, not a live site for the business. Facts above (address, phone, hours) are pulled from public directory listings for reference, not supplied by the business, and the demo itself (including its name and branding) is subject to change before anything is finalized.
