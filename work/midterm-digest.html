Midterm Digest

A side project, not client work: a daily, nonpartisan tracker scoring every confirmed 2026 U.S. Senate, House, and governor race-rating change from Cook Political Report, Sabato's Crystal Ball, and Inside Elections. One point per confirmed move, nothing else counted.

  • TracksSenate, House, governor races
  • SourcesCook, Sabato, Inside Elections
  • CadenceDaily digest, with a searchable archive
Visit the Live Site

The Brief

Score the signal, skip the noise

Polls, fundraising, and forecast odds move the conversation but not the record. Midterm Digest only scores a confirmed rating change from one of three trusted raters, so the running total reflects what's actually shifted, not what's being speculated about. A seat-change chart with a table view, a map of where the moves happened, a searchable archive of every daily issue, and a plain-language explainer of how the score works come with it.
