Iroquois Lanes & Pro Shop

Canajoharie, NY's bowling alley, pro shop, and the attached Ten Pin Grill & Sports Bar. Previously reachable only by phone and scattered directory listings — now with a real site of their own.

The Brief

Book a party without playing phone tag

Bowling alleys make real money on things a website can drive directly: birthday parties, league nights, and glow bowling. The site is built around that — a clear "book your event" path, bowling & leagues info, and the grill's menu treated as a first-class page, not an afterthought.

Facts above (address, phone, hours) were originally pulled from public directory listings for reference before the real site existed. The live site linked above is the finished build.
