Iroquois Lanes & Pro Shop

Canajoharie, NY's bowling alley, pro shop, and the attached Ten Pin Grill & Sports Bar. Currently reachable only by phone and scattered directory listings; no site of their own yet.

  • Location192 Erie Blvd, Canajoharie, NY
  • Phone(518) 673-4464
  • DrawsGlow bowling, birthday parties, lessons, pro shop

The brief

Book a party without playing phone tag

Bowling alleys make real money on things a website can drive directly: birthday parties, league nights, and glow bowling. The plan is a site built around that — a clear "book your event" path, a live-feeling events calendar for glow nights, and the grill's menu treated as a first-class page, not an afterthought.

Facts above (address, phone, hours) are pulled from public directory listings for reference. Final content, photography, and menu details are pending direct input from the business.
