No. 003 Concept

MK Soap

A small-batch soap company currently selling through a Bigcartel storefront. This project redesigns the front-end presentation to match the quality of the product, while keeping checkout on the existing, trusted storefront.

  • Current sitemksoap.com (Bigcartel)
  • ScopeFront-end redesign, checkout stays on Bigcartel

The brief

Presentation worth the product

Hand-poured soap deserves better than a generic storefront template. The plan is a proper front door for the brand — real photography, the story of how it's made, and a design that reads as a considered product line rather than a marketplace listing — while every "buy" button hands off to the existing Bigcartel checkout. No commerce migration, no risk to existing orders.

This project is at the concept stage. Product photography and copy are pending; checkout will continue to run through Bigcartel.
