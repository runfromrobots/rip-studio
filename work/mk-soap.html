MK Soap

A small-batch soap company, hand-pouring marbled and design-forward bars, currently between homes on its way from Watertown, MA to a new base upstate in New York. The new site is the front door while the business is on hiatus — the story, the craft, the photography, ready for when it's back in season.

  • StatusOn hiatus, relocating upstate NY
  • Founded2018
  • ScopeFull site rebuild, no active checkout right now
Visit the Live Site

The Brief

Presentation worth the product

Hand-poured soap deserves better than a generic storefront template. The site is a proper front door for the brand — real photography, the story of how it's made, and a design that reads as a considered product line rather than a marketplace listing. With the business between locations, there's no active checkout right now; the site's job is to hold the brand's ground until it's back.
