The Inaugural Yatesville Creek Paper Boat Race

A side project, not client work: a single-page announcement for a paper boat race down Yatesville Creek in Sprakers, NY — fold a boat, launch it below the falls, and follow it downstream to the finish.

  • LocationYatesville Creek, Sprakers, NY
  • DateJuly 9, 2027 (tentative)
  • EntryInvite-only, by word of mouth
Visit the Live Site

The Brief

Fold. Float. Finish.

No registration platform, no ticketing system, just what a backyard invite-only event actually needs: what it is, when and where, and a spot to leave an e-mail for when registration opens. Three plain steps, a details table, and a stylized map of the creek do the rest of the convincing.
