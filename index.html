Who's Rip

Small batch, no shortcuts

RIP Studio is a one-person shop based in New York's Mohawk Valley, building things meant to last, whether that's a hand-poured bar of soap or a hand-built website. No bloated page builders, no recurring fee to a platform that doesn't know your business.

Behind the name is Michael Kennedy, who's spent 15 years as a UX researcher (mikewkennedy.com) studying why people actually trust, use, or abandon a website. That background is what separates this from a template shop: every site here is built from research instincts, not just good taste.
